About AI Communication Assessment

CareerPrepHub evaluates your written communication from your text answers — not voice, tone, pronunciation, or facial expressions. The assessment covers clarity, answer structure, conciseness, technical vocabulary, and completeness. This gives you actionable feedback on how clearly you express technical concepts in writing.
